Our Philosophy

EVERY PACE,
A STORY.

Traditional photobooths freeze people in stiff poses and predictable props. PACEFRAME was born on city tracks and urban alleyways to disrupt that script.

We merge rapid flash response, commercial-grade cinema sensors, and hyper-responsive modular booths that breathe with your event’s rhythm. Whether it’s a 5K finish line, a subterranean gallery release, or an avant-garde wedding party, we deliver photographs that look like editorial magazine covers.

Zero Hesitation

Candid pacing prompts attendees to move freely rather than freeze.

Bespoke Aesthetics

Custom neon typographic frames engineered uniquely for each organizer.
